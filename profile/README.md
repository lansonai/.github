## About

LANSONAI (Lanson AI) helps creators and teams:

- **Podcast & video transcription** — High-accuracy, multilingual transcription and translation
- **Real-time speech** — Zero-latency capture and instant translation
- **Professional export** — Multiple formats for your workflow

Transcribe, translate, and repurpose podcast and video content with AI—faster and more accurately.
